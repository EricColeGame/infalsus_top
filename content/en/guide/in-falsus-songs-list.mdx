The In Falsus song list is the first screen most players study after clearing the tutorial — and the last one they fully finish. With 70+ tracks in the 1.0 release and more than 40 of them originals, lowiro's rhythm game buries a lot of structure behind a simple scrolling menu. This guide breaks down the difficulty tiers, the chapter system, the DLC pack, the note counts worth chasing, and the charts that separate comfortable clears from genuine walls. Numbers below come from community trackers and player experience, so treat them as a reliable map rather than absolute gospel — note counts shift between builds.

## How the In Falsus Song List Is Organized

Every entry in In Falsus is sorted by chapter, and the chapters map to story progression rather than genre. Community trackers generally group them as Prologue, Act 1, mid-story acts (often labeled Act 2.5 in player discussions), Act 3, and the standalone Virtual Singers tag used for DLC.

Chart levels across the full release span 1 through 15, and that ladder includes a masked FBD 15 entry that community reports describe as a hidden song. That single hidden chart is the ceiling of the entire list.

### The Four Difficulty Tiers

Each song can carry up to four charts. Understanding the key layout matters more than the number next to it, because the jump from four keys to six changes how you read a pattern entirely.

| Chart | Keys | What Makes It Different | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIN (Minimal) | 4K | Static finger placement; the entry difficulty | Every song |
| EVO (Evolved) | 5K | Five lanes, and the three active middle lanes can shift mid-chart | Every song |
| ULT (Ultimate) | 6K | All six keys and lanes active; the standard top difficulty | Every song |
| FBD (Forbidden) | 6K+ | Six keys plus exclusive wide notes | Selected songs only |

The practical takeaway: MIN and EVO teach you a song's rhythm, but they do not prepare your hands for wide notes. If a song has an FBD chart, treat it as a separate skill test, not a harder version of ULT.

## Unlock Routes and Progression Tips

Most of the In Falsus song list unlocks through normal play, but the routes differ by chapter.

| Route | Example Songs | How to Get Them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | Cryogenic, Ghost Ray | Available from the start in the Prologue |
| Story progress | F0 CHA0S, Latent Duality, Memories in Noise | Clear Act 1 story nodes |
| DLC purchase | Monitoring, Roki, Lost One's Weeping | Buy the Virtual Singers Song Pack |
| Secrets | Hidden FBD 15 chart | Community-reported unlock conditions; not automatic |

Four habits that make the climb smoother:

- **Clear MIN first, always.** Four-key charts build muscle memory for a song's rhythm before lane shifts arrive in EVO.
- **Watch the middle lanes in EVO.** The three active middle lanes can move mid-chart, which is the single biggest source of early misses.
- **Do not jump into FBD from a level 8 ULT.** Aim for ULT 10–11 comfort before touching wide notes.
- **Track your note counts, not just your grades.** A clear with 40 misses on a 2,000-note chart usually means an accuracy problem, not a stamina one.

One caution: chart levels for the newest DLC tracks have circulated mainly through community wikis pending in-game verification, so expect small adjustments as more players post clears.

## FAQ

### How many songs are in the In Falsus song list?

The 1.0 release shipped with 70+ songs, more than 40 of them originals. Community trackers have independently verified around 67 entries with chart data, and the total climbs as new content and DLC land. Fan pages sometimes cite 75+ tracks once the Virtual Singers Song Pack is counted.

### What do MIN, EVO, ULT, and FBD mean?

MIN is the four-key entry chart. EVO uses five keys with shifting middle lanes. ULT activates all six keys and serves as the standard top difficulty. FBD adds six keys plus exclusive wide notes and only exists on selected songs.

### What is the hardest chart in the game?

By chart level, a hidden FBD 15 song sits at the very top, with code:Leviathan [FBD 14] just beneath it. Among named charts, several Act 2.5 and Act 3 entries reach FBD 13, and Roki [FBD] carries 2,583 measured notes — the densest count on the In Falsus song list.

### Do I need the DLC to see every track?

Yes. The five Virtual Singers tracks are sold as a separate pack and are tagged under their own chapter, so they will not appear in your song select until you own them.

### Is Cryogenic playable outside In Falsus?

It is, according to community reports — it has appeared in several other rhythm games, making it the most widely exported track from the roster.
