## What MIN, EVO, ULT, and FBD Actually Mean

New players often assume the four labels are just difficulty names. In practice they define how the In Falsus OST is consumed, because each tier re-charts the same audio file rather than simply speeding it up.

| Tier | Role | Example Levels (Community Data) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIN | Entry tier, readable for newcomers | 2–4 |
| EVO | Mid tier, the "standard" clear | 5–7 |
| ULT | High tier for experienced players | 9–10 |
| FBD | Locked premium tier, unlocked over time | 11–12 |

The FBD slot appears locked on the song select screen until players meet its conditions, which is why community trackers mark it separately from the standard three. Note counts scale sharply across tiers. Altered Edge, for example, runs 378 notes on MIN and 1,435 on FBD — nearly a fourfold increase in density on the same track.

For anyone exploring the In Falsus OST as a listener rather than a player, this tier system is also a useful difficulty guide. If a song's ULT chart sits at level 10, you are almost certainly looking at dense, layered production with aggressive percussion.

## Tips for Getting More Out of the Soundtrack

A few habits make the In Falsus OST easier to enjoy, whether you are chasing full clears or just building a playlist.

- **Start on MIN, then jump to FBD for the same song.** Hearing one track at both extremes shows how much arrangement changes between tiers.
- **Follow the arcs, not the alphabet.** Songs are grouped by story arc, and playing an arc in order keeps the soundtrack's pacing intact.
- **Check note counts before you commit.** A 1,400-note FBD chart is a very different time investment than a 380-note MIN chart.
- **Do not skip the DLC.** The Virtual Singers pack adds five full tracks with complete chart sets, not filler.
- **Watch official showcases for previews.** They are the most reliable way to hear unreleased or newly added material early.
- **Track community data with a grain of salt.** Fan wikis are updated by volunteers, so levels and note counts can shift after patches.
