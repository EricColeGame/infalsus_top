## Tips for Clearing High-FBD code: Charts

Clearing an FBD 13 or FBD 14 chart is a different skill from clearing a standard chart. Here's what community chart analysis suggests works.

- **Drop your speed first.** Charts like the FBD 12 entry are tuned around a specific speed readout. If your speed is far off, note density becomes unreadable before difficulty even matters.
- **Learn the chart in sections.** Frame-by-frame breakdowns exist precisely because these charts have distinct walls. Identify your wall, then practice around it rather than restarting from zero.
- **Respect the Anomaly modifier.** An FBD 14 under Anomaly behaves differently from the same chart in standard conditions. Don't assume your muscle memory transfers.
- **Check your audio latency before blaming the chart.** A 20–30 ms offset that's harmless on lower tiers becomes brutal at FBD 14.
- **Take breaks between attempts.** High-FBD charts punish autopilot. Three focused attempts beat fifteen sloppy ones.

## Common Problems When Hunting In Falsus Codes

A few recurring issues show up in community reports from players trying to reach **In Falsus codes** content.

**The song won't appear at all.** In most reported cases, the player hasn't finished the prerequisite progression. Because `code:` entries don't always trigger a notification, players assume the unlock failed when it actually landed silently. Re-scan your full song list, including any "extra" or "special" tabs.

**A website promised working codes.** No verified redeem-code system exists. Anything claiming otherwise is almost certainly a survey trap. For official news, check the game's Steam page and the developers' own announcements.

**The Anomaly version is missing.** Anomaly variants are tied to progression and encounter state. If the base chart is available but the variant isn't, you're likely missing an encounter or card step.
