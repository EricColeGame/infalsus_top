## Frequently Asked Questions About the In Falsus Price

**How much does In Falsus cost?**
The base game is listed at $39.99 on Steam as a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, season pass, or premium currency, so the In Falsus price you see at checkout is the full cost of the core game.

**Does the base price include the DLC song packs?**
No. The Virtual Singers Song Pack is priced separately at $5.99, and the CHUNITHM Collaboration Song Pack had no published price at launch. Neither pack is required to finish the story or use the crafting system.

**Is In Falsus worth $39.99?**
For players who enjoy rhythm games with a heavy narrative component, the math is favorable: over 70 songs, four difficulties each, five story routes, and a card-crafting layer for about $0.57 per track. The "Very Positive" Steam rating from more than 1,200 reviews supports that assessment, though the difficulty ceiling may not suit everyone.

**Are there cheaper ways to play?**
Yes. Wishlisting the game and waiting for a Steam seasonal sale is the most reliable route, and Family Sharing lets a household member try it without a second purchase. Both options depend on timing and account setup, so check current terms before counting on them.
