## What You Actually Play: Rhythm Woven Into Narrative

In Falsus is not a rhythm game with a story bolted on. The two halves feed each other. Songs are tied to specific encounters in the scenario, and the characters you meet along the way become the artwork on the cards you craft.

The core loop works like this:

1. **Clear songs** across four difficulty tiers to earn materials.
2. **Collect Iota**, the game's core crafting resource.
3. **Slot Iota into Blueprint grids**, which are puzzle-like layouts where placement changes the outcome.
4. **Craft Cards** from those blueprints, then equip up to five of them.
5. **Return to the music** with new advantages against tougher encounters.

Because each Blueprint is a puzzle rather than a fixed recipe, two players can chase the same card and end up with meaningfully different results. That's the design hook that separates In Falsus from a straightforward track-clearing game.
