### Songs, Difficulties, and the Field

Every story encounter is tied to a specific song, so the soundtrack doubles as a narrative device. Composers from Japan's independent music scene and international artists contributed to the lineup, spanning multiple genres rather than locking into one style. The four difficulty tiers exist to carry players from "flow" to "mastery," and the aerial Field adds a layer of spatial movement on top of standard note-reading — a detail that separated the game from more conventional lane-based rhythm titles in early impressions.

### Blueprints, Iota, and Cards

The crafting loop is the piece most players underestimate at first. Clearing songs earns Iota, which you slot into Blueprint grids. The grid is a puzzle, not a menu: where you place materials changes the resulting Card's stats and effects, so two players with identical materials can end up with different Cards. Since a loadout caps at five Cards, deck decisions matter more than raw grinding.
