## What an In Falsus Demo Would Actually Need to Show

Understanding the systems explains why a trial is hard to build well. In Falsus ties rhythm gameplay directly to its story: encounters with other characters are scored by unique songs, and clearing those songs feeds materials back into your loadout. A demo that stripped the narrative away would misrepresent the game; one that kept it would spoil early plot beats.

The progression loop is the other obstacle. You clear songs to collect Iota, the game's core crafting material, then slot Iota into Blueprint grids. Blueprints produce Cards, and how you complete a grid changes the resulting Card's effects. You can equip up to five Cards at once, which reshapes how you approach tougher songs later on. Sampling that loop properly takes more than a single track.
